const ExactCard & card )

Rotates a card's art upright when its layout shows sideways.

Sideways-layout cards (planes, sieges/battles, split cards) store their landscape artwork rotated 90° inside a portrait frame. Art-crop displays, playmat art, and the card-info picture must show such art upright before sampling or painting. Portrait cards are returned unchanged.

Parameters
artThe card pixmap to orient.
cardThe card describing the art orientation.
Returns
art rotated 90° clockwise for sideways-layout cards, else art.
