Detailed Description

Picks the two colors of a filled treatment's vertical gradient.

The pre-#7595 icons were filled with a lighter top over a darker bottom. The badge and the sticker share one fill rule so they sit next to each other with the same green or red.

The authored tone is the top of that gradient: the palette author stores one color per emphasis level, so the treatment only adds depth by darkening the bottom. Hue, saturation and value all stay the author's, so the rendered ladder is the authored ladder on every scheme.

◆ Scheme

enum class ThemeIconFill::Scheme
strong

Which scheme a treatment is painted for.

The scheme is a property of the surface, not of the tone: an authored quiet level is deliberately darker than its secondary level on the dark scheme too, so it cannot be inferred from the tone's value. Callers know the surface palette and pass it explicitly.
