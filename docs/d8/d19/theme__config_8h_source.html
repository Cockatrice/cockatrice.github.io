12// Application-specific color roles, layered on top of the fixed QPalette role
13// set. Stored in the same palette-<scheme>.toml under an [AppColors] section
14// and editable from the palette editor, so theme authors can control colors
15// beyond what Qt's palette can express.
16//
17// The icon families carry one role per emphasis level. Icon is the neutral
18// glyph ink; IconStrong and IconSoft are its primary and quiet levels. Positive,
19// Negative, Informational, Hidden, Marked and Movement follow the same pattern,
20// so an emphasis never has to be computed from a hue at paint time: the theme
21// authors all three levels. Naming a variant explicitly (`IconStrong`) pins that
22// level; naming the family (`Icon`) lets the caller's emphasis choose.
23namespace AppColor

25Q_NAMESPACE
