Detailed Description

Paints the sticker treatment of a currentColor glyph.

The pre-#7595 add, remove and delete icons were filled shapes with a black outline and no disc. A tint alone cannot reproduce that: it produces a hairline, and a badge disc is a different icon language. The sticker treatment rebuilds the look from the same sentinel art by drawing the glyph twice, once with a dark outline and once with a vertical tone gradient on top.

A sticker is louder than a line, which suits an add or a destructive action. It needs a size where the tone band is at least two device pixels wide. Callers opt in. Navigation and menus keep the line treatment.

Everything here works in device pixels, like the badge treatment: the engine renders at the size it already computed and attaches the device pixel ratio afterwards.

QColor ThemeIconSticker::outlineColor ( const QColor & tone)

Returns the dark rim color the sticker is painted with.

The old art had a black outline. Deriving it from the tone reads as black on a light scheme without a literal, and stays visible on a dark one, where a pure black rim would disappear into the window.

QPixmap ThemeIconSticker::renderSticker ( const QByteArray & svg,
const QColor & tone,
const QSize & pixelSize )

Renders a sentinel-carrying SVG as a filled glyph with a black outline.

The glyph is drawn larger than the canvas so the padded sentinel paths fill the icon the way the old art did, with a vertical tone gradient as the fill and a near-black rim. Authored art is never drawn through this path.

Parameters
svgSentinel-carrying SVG source; currentColor is resolved internally.
toneGlyph color, already resolved for the icon mode.
pixelSizeCanvas size in device pixels; a non-square canvas centers the glyph and leaves the remaining area transparent.
Returns
The composited sticker, or a null pixmap for empty or invalid input.
