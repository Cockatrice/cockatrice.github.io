Detailed Description

Manages the game board display including players, zones, cards, and animations.

GameScene handles:

  • Dynamic arrangement of players in columns/rows.
  • Player rotation adjustments.
  • Zone views for cards (e.g., graveyard, library, revealed zones).
  • Hover and animation handling for cards.
  • Scene resizing and responsive layout.

◆ rearrange()

void GameScene::rearrange ( )

Recomputes the layout of players and the scene size.

Steps:

  1. Collect active players who haven't conceded.
  2. Rotate player list based on first local player and rotation offset.
  3. Determine number of columns.
  4. Compute scene size and layout.
  5. Update toolbar height and scene rectangle.
  6. Adjust columns and player positions to match view size.

◆ hoverLiftedAncestors

QList<QPair<QPointer<QGraphicsObject>, qreal> > GameScene::hoverLiftedAncestors
private

Ancestor chain of the hovered card with pre-hover Z-values. Lifted while hovering because Qt orders subtrees level by level: the card z alone cannot beat a sibling zone, and the top ancestor alone cannot beat same-area sibling zones either — every level from the zone up must win its own comparison. Guarded pointers: if the card is destroyed mid-hover (or an ancestor goes away first), the guards null out and the next restore skips them instead of touching freed memory. An orphaned lift is invisible — areas and zones tile without overlapping and all UI layers still win — and any outstanding lift is restored by the next hover transition.
